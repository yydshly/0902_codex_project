### Interaction refinement

- P2: dragging could be interpreted as a click and select the room under the release point.
- Fix: added pointer-distance tracking and suppressed selection after a drag gesture.
- P2: labels could drift into the right detail card during guided focus.
- Fix: projected label positions are clamped to a card-aware safe area.
- P2: the initial runtime reported deprecated Three.js timing/shadow APIs.
- Fix: moved timing to `THREE.Timer` and the supported shadow-map configuration; a new clean browser tab has no warnings.
